About this draft

Concept

BrainSail is a small game about the assumption that a problem has only one right way to be solved or has to be logical. You sail to rescue your princess while storms loop across the sea. Each storm that hits you opens a “Brainstorm”: a problem and an unconventional approach, using random words. Pick a word, use it to spark a new idea, and decide whether your answer is clear. It plays with Edward de Bono’s lateral thinking: a random word can open a path you would never have taken. Looking forward to your feedback so I can refine it and make it better. Tell me what worked, what was not working, and what you’re not sure about or have questions about.

Controls

Press Start. Steer with the arrow keys or the on-screen triangles. Pause and Restart are on the sea map. In a Brainstorm, pick a word, type your idea, then press Enter or wait for the clock. Turn sound on with the sound switch.

Current version

This is a first draft. What works: three levels with more storms, fewer words, and less time; the Brainstorm with a clear check; Pause and Restart; a rare Easter egg (see if you can find it!!!); sound and meditative music; and a phone layout.
